Pearl Payjoin

Two wallets, one transaction. The receiver's input joins the sender's payment in a single Taproot transaction — breaking the common-input-ownership heuristic, on Pearl rails.

Honest limits

  • This is a lab: it builds the exact BIP-78 offer a server would serve, but it is not a server. A production receiver endpoint must also authenticate the sender and rate-limit requests.
  • Only PRL Taproot (prl1…) addresses are accepted — never testnet or foreign prefixes.

Sender — validate, fund, sign the original

Paste the receiver's offer. The desk validates the template, then you fund it with your own UTXOs, pay an exact fee, and sign + finalize every input — that signed original is what BIP-78 says you hand to the receiver.

Proposal — the receiver answers

Paste the sender's signed original. The desk re-verifies the sender's signatures, then the receiver adds their own input (signed + finalized), inserts it at a random position, and computes the exact fee split. Deductions from the payment output are bounded by maxadditionalfeecontribution — anything more is refused, loudly.

Verify — the sender's checklist

Paste the receiver's proposal (the original is carried over from the earlier tabs). The desk runs the full BIP-78 sender checklist — 15 checks, each PASS/FAIL with the exact reason. A failing proposal must never be signed.

Sign — finalize and broadcast

The proposal is re-verified first — verification failure refuses to sign, no exceptions. Then your sender inputs are re-signed with fresh SIGHASH_DEFAULT signatures and every input is finalized. Out comes the broadcast-ready transaction.

What this is, honestly

  • A BIP-78 lab, not a wallet and not a server. It performs the exact sender/receiver protocol steps with real cryptography (BIP-340/341, BIP-86, BIP-174) and refuses protocol violations loudly — but there is no payjoin endpoint here, no coin selection beyond what you enter, and no privacy beyond what one joined transaction buys you.
  • Keys never leave the page. Mnemonics you type are used in-memory only. Wipe them when done. Nothing is stored: no localStorage, no cookies, no analytics.
  • Network calls are opt-in and labeled. UTXO lookups, fee estimates, and broadcast use the public Pearl blockbook and are marked live where they appear.
  • Broadcast is double-gated. The broadcast button stays disabled until you check the explicit confirmation box.
