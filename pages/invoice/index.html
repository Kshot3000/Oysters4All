⚖ Honest limits

  
    
    
  • Receive-only. This page never spends PRL and never needs the invoicer's keys. The mnemonic tool only derives receiving addresses.
    • 
    
  • No custody, no escrow. Payment happens on-chain, peer to peer. The descriptor proves the invoice existed, not that anyone was paid.
    • 
    
  • Watch ≠ settlement. 0-confirmation payments are shown flagged ⚠ unconfirmed. Wait for 1+ confirmations before treating an invoice as settled.
    • 
    
  • Blockbook is a third party. The watch reads a Blockbook you configure; if it's down, stale, or lying, the page says so instead of inventing data.
    • 
    
  • Descriptors are commitments, not signatures. They bind invoice content to a hash — they don't prove who issued the invoice.
    • 
    
  • Due dates are advisory. They are sealed into the descriptor but nothing on-chain enforces them.
