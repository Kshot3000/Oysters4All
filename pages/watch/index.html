Honest limits — read before trusting the lamp

  • Read-only, forever. Pearl Watch never asks for keys, seeds, or passwords. It only watches public addresses.
  • Blockbook is third-party. Balances, txs and confirmations come from a Blockbook server you choose (default: https://blockbook.pearlresearch.ai). It is convenience data, not consensus — verify anything that matters against your own pearld.
  • Polling only. This page polls on a timer you set. It cannot push alerts to your phone and it only watches while the tab is open (a closed tab sees nothing).
  • Amounts are heuristics. Incoming/outgoing attribution reads Blockbook's vin/vout address lists; totals are grain-exact math on that third-party data.
  • No smart contracts on Pearl. Rules fire in your browser; nothing on-chain enforces them.
  • Watchlist lives in this browser (localStorage). Export it before switching devices.
