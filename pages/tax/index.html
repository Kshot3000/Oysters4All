        Honest limits

        • Not tax advice. This is a bookkeeping aid. Crypto tax rules differ by jurisdiction and change — have a professional review the output before filing.
        • FIFO only. No HIFO, LIFO, or specific identification. Every disposal consumes your oldest lots first.
        • Prices are yours. There is no live price oracle; every USD figure derives from prices you entered. Wrong prices in, wrong gains out.
        • Fees reduce the net. Network fees are disposed at zero proceeds — their basis reduces net gain / increases losses, and is also shown separately as “Fee basis consumed”.
        • Gifts are simplified. Gifts received take fair-market-value basis at receipt; gifts given recognize no gain and claim no loss — the lots simply leave the books. Real carryover-basis rules may differ where you file.
        • Transfers need their history. A transfer-in is booked at receipt-date FMV unless you set the original acquisition date — set it to preserve the true holding period. A transfer-out carries its basis to the other wallet with no taxable event here.
        • Sends are netted; true mixes split. A send with change is modeled on its net outflow only — the change keeps its original lots. A tx co-signed by outside money is split into receive + send legs for you to classify.
        • Unconfirmed transactions are excluded from the ledger and flagged in the import list.
        • Read-only. The page never holds keys, never signs, never broadcasts. It cannot move your PRL.
