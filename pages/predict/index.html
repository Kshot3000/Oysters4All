Pearl Predict

Prediction markets on Pearl Taproot — no smart contracts. Define a market, get one Taproot funding address per outcome, let traders fund their side, and the arbiter settles with a single proportional-payout transaction. Every market carries a tamper-evident descriptor; every payout carries re-verifiable Schnorr signatures. Five steps: Create → Fund → Resolve → Track → Verify.

Honest limits — always true:
  • The arbiter is trusted to sign honest payouts. The scripts prove the arbiter authorized a payout — they cannot prove the payout was correct. Choose an arbiter everyone accepts, or don't play.
  • Positions are manually tracked: the desk records the txids you paste or import. It cannot see funding transactions you never tell it about.
  • No live market is running here — there is no order book, no odds feed, no automatic settlement. Funding, signing, and broadcasting are deliberate, human-confirmed actions.
  • Blockbook is a third-party read source. The page never moves real PRL without your keys and an explicit confirmation click.

Publish checklist

  1. Post the descriptor where traders can see it (forum, chat, Nostr).
  2. Post the canonical market JSON (download above) — anyone can recompute the descriptor from it.
  3. Traders verify: same descriptor from the JSON, addresses re-derive (Verify tab).
  4. Funding is only valid before the trading deadline — late funds are at the trader's own risk.

Descriptor pearl-predict:v1:<hrp>:<marketHash>:<nOutcomes>:<tradeH>:<resolveH>, marketHash = SHA-256 over the canonical market JSON. Taproot addresses use a NUMS internal key (H("PearlPredictNUMS/v1"‖marketHash‖outcomeIndex)) — nobody knows the private key, so coins move only through the two script leaves.

Track positions

Traders fund their chosen outcome's address. Record each funding here — paste a funding txid and let the desk read it off Blockbook (GET-only), or enter a position by hand. Payout addresses are recorded per position; the chain cannot tell us where a trader wants to be paid, so confirm them with the trader.

Pot totals and implied probabilities update as positions are recorded. Pot = Σ positions on that outcome. Implied P = pot ÷ total pot.

Award payout

The arbiter picks the winning outcome. The desk builds one transaction spending every tracked funding UTXO (winners' and losers' pots alike) and paying each winning-outcome funder stake × totalPot ÷ winningPot, minus the fee, grain-exact. The arbiter signs each input locally; every signature is re-verified before it enters the bundle.

Void path

If the market cannot resolve (ambiguous event, bad source), the refund leaf sweeps every pot back to its recorded funder after the resolution deadline: nLockTime = resolveH, stake minus a pro-rata fee share. Signed by the refund sweep key.

Standalone verifier
