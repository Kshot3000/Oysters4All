📖 Sources & honest limits

  • Emission math — exact replica of CalcBlockSubsidy in node/blockchain/validate.go (pearl-research-labs/pearl @ 3fe2267): total supply 2.1B PRL, emission constant 650,226 blocks, 194 s block target, 100-block coinbase maturity. Verified against upstream emission_test.go vectors in the test suite.
  • CMP 90HX preset — 180 W/GPU wall (179.47 / 179.55 W sampled), 2-card rig 358–360 W, from docs/rig-benchmarks.md in chernuha-dev/miner-cpm90hx-pearl (2026-09-27). Its 5-minute pool comparison (Forge 7 accepted shares vs 1) is explicitly labeled noisy by the author — use it for power, not yield.
  • Yield preset 0.0241 PRL/TH/day — spark-pearl-miner docs/en/VIABILITY.md (2026-09-26), derived from hashrate.no; cross-checks against their reported 80 TH/s → 1.85–1.94 PRL/day (HeroMiners). Drifts with difficulty and subsidy.
  • Price — CoinGecko pearl-2 (aggregated spot, CORS-open) by default; CoinEx PEARLUSDT alternate (the L1's real market — PRLUSDT is a different token).
  • Limits — no network hashrate or difficulty feed is modeled; measured pool output is always more reliable than TH/s estimates.
