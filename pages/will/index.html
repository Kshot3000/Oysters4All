Honest limits — read before drafting

  • This page enforces routing, not intent: the scripts guarantee who can move the coins and when — they cannot verify that your heirs are the right people. Key custody is on you.
  • Unlock is a block height, not a date: heirs can claim at/after the unlock block (CLTV-gated, enforced by consensus). The "~days" estimates use Pearl's ≈194 s block target and drift.
  • The owner can always move the funds — until the moment the heirs claim them. There is no dead-man's switch on-chain; if you spend the vault, the heirs' claim becomes worthless. Tell them how to watch the vault.
  • Not legal advice: this is a key-management tool, not a legal will. No probate, no executor, no estate law — just Taproot.
  • This page never holds your keys: mnemonics and private keys live in this page's memory only and are never sent anywhere. Broadcasts go through Blockbook only with your double-confirm.

Draft the vault

Two leaves under a NUMS internal key (nobody's key — no keypath backdoor): the owner leaf (<owner> CHECKSIG, spendable any time) and the heir leaf (<unlock> CLTV DROP m-of-n CHECKSIGADD, spendable only at/after the unlock block).

Owner key

The owner reclaims at any time. Paste a 64-hex x-only pubkey, or derive one locally.

Heirs (1–5)

Each heir contributes an x-only pubkey. Heir keys are sorted lexicographically in the script, so the vault is canonical — the same descriptor always rebuilds the same address, byte for byte.
