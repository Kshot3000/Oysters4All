The register

Every prl-name inscription the configured indexer knows about, resolved to current owners by first-seen consensus: the lowest inscription number wins, transfers only count from the current owner, releases free the handle for re-claim.

The scan walks the indexer's asc-ordered /inscriptions list (100/page, capped above) and is a sample of indexed data — it is only as complete as the indexer you point it at. Ownership = first-seen per that indexer's ordering.

Registrar's notice — honest limits

  • prl-name is an app-level convention, not Pearl consensus. Pearl has no smart contracts; nothing on-chain enforces handle ownership. Two indexers can disagree.
  • Ownership = first-seen per the indexer's ordering. Resolution depends entirely on the indexer you configure (unconfigured by default — supply your own, per Gallery precedent).
  • Claiming costs real PRL in commit/reveal chain fees. There is no refund.
  • Never claim legal rights to a handle. A registry entry is a social signal, not a trademark.
  • No squatting protection beyond first-seen. The wizard checks the last directory scan, but someone else's inscription can land first.
