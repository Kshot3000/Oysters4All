Honest limits

  • Nothing is locked until the issuer actually sends PRL to each tranche address — the term sheet is a plan until funded.
  • Coupon dates are block-height estimates (194 s target). Real maturity follows the chain, not the calendar.
  • Secondary transfers need the seller's cooperation to presign; there is no trustless listing or price discovery here.
  • Tracking reads the public Blockbook indexer. Balances shown are on-chain facts, not promises.
  • Test on testnet first. This is a fixed-income toolkit, not legal, tax, or investment advice.
