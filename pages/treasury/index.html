Pearl · Treasury Desk

Pearl Treasury

The vault ledger for teams and DAOs holding PRL. Register m-of-n Taproot vaults, draft spend proposals with tamper-evident ids, collect cosigner signatures to quorum, and disburse real Taproot transactions — with an append-only, hash-chained audit trail for every move. No smart contracts on Pearl; pure Taproot script, keys never leave this page.

Honest limits — always true:
  • Pearl has no smart contracts: this desk coordinates off-chain. Proposals and signatures are local records until a quorum-signed transaction is broadcast — the chain only ever sees the final Taproot spend.
  • Balances and fee estimates read Blockbook, a third-party indexer — it can lag or be wrong. The UTXO you paste for a disbursement is your responsibility to confirm.
  • Private keys live in memory only, never in localStorage. Vault descriptors, proposals, and the audit log are public metadata and do persist locally.
  • Recurring schedules are a calendar table in this browser — nothing on-chain enforces timing; each payout is a manual quorum spend when due.
  • No real funds moved in testing — disbursement hex is real transaction construction; verify it before you broadcast.

Vaults

Register m-of-n Taproot vaults with a covenant:v1:<hrp>:<m>-of-<n>:<keys…> descriptor. The address is fully determined by the cosigner set — no keypath bypass exists (NUMS internal key). Registering is watch-only: no keys required.

Spend proposals

Draft a spend intent. The proposal id is the SHA-256 of its canonical JSON — tamper with any field and the id stops matching. Amounts use integer grain math only; the payee must be a valid Taproot address on the vault's network.

Cosigner signing

    
Turn a proposal into a real Taproot signing round, sign it locally with
      a cosigner key, and pass the round JSON to the other cosigners. Every signature is
      Schnorr-verified against the round digest before it is stored; a key that is
      not a cosigner is loudly refused.

Disburse

    
When a proposal's round reaches quorum, finalize the real Taproot spend,
      inspect the raw hex, and broadcast it through Blockbook. The round is checked
      against the proposal intent first — a round that drifts from the approved payee or
      amount is refused.
