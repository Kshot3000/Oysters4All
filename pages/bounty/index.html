Pearl Bounty

Post a PRL bounty for a bug, a task, a feature. The reward sits in a Taproot output only your signature can move — award it to the winning hunter, or reclaim it yourself after the deadline. Hunters commit to solutions with SHA-256 so nobody can steal a reveal. Pure Taproot, no smart contracts, keys never leave this page.

Honest limits — always true:
  • A bounty is poster-trusted: script cannot judge solution quality — the poster's signature is the award decision. The trustless direction is the reclaim: hunters never risk funds, only the poster funds the output.
  • Submissions live in this browser only (localStorage). There is no listing relay — publish the bounty descriptor wherever hunters gather.
  • The deadline is an absolute block height. Day-estimates use ≈194 s per block; the chain decides, not the clock.
  • Funding and status checks read Blockbook, a third-party indexer — it can lag or be wrong.
  • No real bounty was funded in testing (that needs the poster's funds and a live chain). Everything here is real cryptography and real transaction construction — verify the hex before you broadcast.

Hunt — commit & reveal

Hunters: commit to your solution before revealing it. The commitment binds your handle, the bounty descriptor, the solution, and a random salt — a copycat can't replay your reveal under their own name. Keep the solution + salt secret until you reveal to the poster out-of-band.

Award the hunter

The poster's signature is the award decision. Spend one bounty UTXO: pay the winner (full pot or a partial award — the remainder returns to your change address). The signature is re-verified before the hex is shown.

Reclaim after the deadline

Past the deadline height, the poster can unilaterally reclaim the pot through the CLTV leaf (nLockTime = deadline). Refused loudly before the deadline — a premature reclaim is invalid on-chain.

Verify
