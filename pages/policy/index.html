Learn — what a policy tree proves (and doesn't)

What Taproot policy trees prove

  • Authorization paths. A tree commits to a fixed set of leaves; each leaf is a script that says who can move the coins and under what on-chain conditions (a signature, a block height, m-of-n keys).
  • Deterministic addresses. The P2TR address is a pure function of the internal key and the leaf scripts. Anyone with the descriptor recomputes the identical address — no registry, no server.
  • Unspendable keypaths, by construction. With a NUMS internal key, nobody knows the discrete log, so the keypath cannot be used. Coins move only through the script leaves you drafted.
  • Tamper evidence. Change one bit of one leaf and the address changes. The Verify tab turns that property into a standalone PROVEN / NOT PROVEN check.

What they don't prove

  • Why the coins should move. Scripts authorize keys; they know nothing about prices, invoices, identities, or any off-chain fact. Authorization is not endorsement.
  • Off-chain reality. Pearl script cannot check a bank transfer, a delivery, or a price feed. Anything real-world must be verified off-chain (see Pearl Hedge's strike-payment check for the pattern).
  • That custom scripts are safe. Expert-mode hex is yours alone — audit it, test it on testnet, never fund what you can't read.
  • Funding. An address is just an address until someone funds it. This page never touches funds; fund from Pearl Sign or Pearl Vault, and double-check the address character by character.

Reading a blueprint

  • The descriptor (pearl-policy:v1:<hrp>:<source>:<leaves>) is the whole design in one line — every field that moves the address is in it.
  • A control block is a leaf's proof of membership in the tree: the internal key plus the sibling hashes up to the root. Paste it into Pearl Sign to spend via that leaf.
  • The sealed commitment (pearl-policy:v1:<hrp>:<sha256(descriptor)>) binds the full descriptor into 32 bytes — publish it to commit to a policy before revealing the leaves.

Honest limits

  • This page never moves PRL and never broadcasts a transaction. It computes addresses and data — funding and spending happen in Pearl Sign or your wallet.
  • A NUMS internal key is intentionally unspendable on the keypath. Lose every leaf key and the funds are unspendable — treat that as permanent.
  • Templates are authorization-only: they prove who can move coins, never why, never at what price, never any off-chain fact.
  • Expert custom scripts are unreviewed. Verify them yourself; never fund a tree you can't fully read.
  • No new cryptography: BIP-340, BIP-341 digests, taggedHash, bech32m — all audited Sign/Escrow/Bounty/Covenant lineage. The desk runs fully offline; it makes zero network requests.
