Pearl Circle

N members put in C PRL a round, for N rounds. Each round one member takes the whole pot — the payout order is drawn by a commit-reveal lottery nobody can game, and every pot sits in a Taproot escrow with a winner-claim leaf and an m-of-n timeout-refund leaf. Pure Taproot, no smart contracts, keys never leave this page.

Honest limits — always true:
  • Contributions are voluntary: this page cannot force any member to fund a round.
  • The order lottery needs all reveals. If a member withholds theirs, the order cannot be computed — the abort path is to refund any funded rounds through the timeout leaf once its lock height passes.
  • Round and lock "times" are block-height estimates (≈194 s per block); the chain decides, not the clock.
  • Funding and status checks read Blockbook, a third-party indexer — it can lag or be wrong. The page cannot tell you who funded a round, only that an address holds funds.
  • Refunds need m of n member signatures on one template. With m < n a coalition can refund early; with m = n one uncooperative member blocks refunds. Choose m with eyes open.

Commit-reveal lottery

Each member generates a random 32-byte secret and publishes sha256(secret). Once every commitment is public, secrets are revealed and the payout order is computed deterministically — late reveals cannot change anyone else's odds because every commitment was already locked in.
