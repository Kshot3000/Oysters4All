Draw the winners

With the settlement block hash in hand, the draw is a pure function: winnerIndex = uint256(SHA-256("raffle-draw:v1:⟨descriptorHash⟩:⟨blockHash⟩:⟨round⟩")) mod totalTickets, mapped onto the weighted ticket ranges. No secrets, no randomness you must trust — anyone can redo every step by hand.

Honest limits

  • No PRL ever moves through this page. It forges commitments, draws winners, verifies draws, and plans payouts — prize custody and the actual sends stay with the organizer's own wallet.
  • Fairness is a commitment protocol, not code: Pearl has no smart contracts, so nothing on-chain enforces the draw. The guarantee is that the descriptor must be published before the settlement block — anyone can then check the entriesHash and redo the SHA-256 draw by hand. A commitment that appears only after the settlement block proves nothing.
  • The beacon is only as unpredictable as the settlement block. Use a block far enough out that no entrant (including the organizer) can influence or predict its hash; the app enforces a ≥ 4-block lead at forge time.
  • Blockbook is a third-party read source. For the draw that decides real money, confirm the settlement block hash with your own pearld node (getblockhash) or a Blockbook you trust — the air-gapped paste path exists for exactly this.
  • No draw runs without the settlement block hash and a matching entry list. If either is missing or tampered, the app refuses — it never invents a winner.
  • Block countdowns assume the 194-second target block time; real block times vary.
