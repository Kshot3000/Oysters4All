The deal

A hash-time-locked contract (HTLC) locks PRL for a claimer who knows the secret preimage. If the swap dies, the refundee sweeps everything back after the timeout. No chain on Pearl runs contracts — this is pure Taproot script, enforced by consensus.

Honest limits

  • Pearl has no smart contracts — this HTLC is Taproot script, and the other chain's leg is entirely the counterparty's responsibility. This page cannot see it, fund it, or enforce it.
  • Timeout ordering is on you: whoever locks first must use the longer refund timeout. The app warns, but it cannot verify the other chain.
  • The maker must keep the preimage secret until they claim the counterparty leg — anyone holding the preimage can claim the PRL.
  • A refund is impossible before the timeout height — that is consensus, not a UI choice.
  • No real swaps were executed while testing this app (needs a live counterparty and funds on both chains).
  • Blockbook reads are GET-only until you press Broadcast; a pasted outpoint is trusted as-is.
  • Saved deals and secrets live in this browser's localStorage — use Wipe, and never reuse a revealed secret.
