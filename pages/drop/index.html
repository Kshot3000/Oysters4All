Pearl · Airdrop Desk

💧 Pearl Drop

The raindrop-storm desk for PRL-20 airdrops. Define a campaign, paste a recipient list, hash it into a Merkle tree, seal a tamper-evident drop descriptor, and let anyone verify their claim — all in your browser, all client-side. This desk never holds funds and never moves PRL: allocations are pledged amounts, distribution is a manual transfer by the operator.

        6 · Methodology & honest limits
        
    
        What this desk does
        
    
          
      
        • Campaign header — canonical JSON, fixed field order
        v,name,tick,starts_at,ends_at,contact; campaign id = SHA-256 of those bytes.
          • 
      
        • Leaf format — drop-v1:<index>:<address>:<amountGrains>,
        UTF-8, index 0-based after dedupe. Amounts are exact BigInt grains (1 PRL = 108 grains).
          • 
      
        • Pair-sorting convention — each parent is
        SHA-256(min‖max) over the lexicographically sorted child pair;
        an odd level duplicates its last leaf. Proofs carry the sibling at each level.
          • 
      
        • Address validation — BIP-350 bech32m, HRP prl, witness
        version 1, 32-byte program, canonical re-encode — same rules as the audited
        Names desk core.
          • 
      
        • Seal document — fixed field order
        v,campaign_id,tick,merkle_root,leaf_count,total_grains,sealed_at,campaign;
        seal id = SHA-256 of the canonical bytes. The embedded full header lets anyone
        re-derive campaign_id without trusting it.
          • 
      
        • Inscription — the seal is inscribed under marker prl-drop
        (never prl-20). Preparing the envelope is this desk's job;
        commit/reveal happens in Pearl Etch.
          • 
    
        
    
        Crypto lineage
        
    
          
      
        • No new cryptography. SHA-256 is the byte-identical
        snipSha256Bytes from the Paywall desk, a mechanical minimization of the
        audited Sign-core @noble/hashes/sha256 — differentially tested there and
        pinned to known vectors here. bech32m is copied verbatim from the audited
        sign/src/crypto.js (from pearlpurse).
          • 
    
        
  
        

        


        
  
        
    Honest limits — always true:
    
          
      
        • This desk never holds funds. Allocations are pledged amounts; distribution is a
        manual PRL-20 transfer by the operator — Pearl has no smart contracts to escrow with.
          • 
      
        • Campaign state and the recipient directory live in your browser's localStorage only;
        nothing here is published or shared.
          • 
      
        • No public prl-drop claim indexer exists yet, so redemptions
        are verified off-chain — claimants check their own proof against the sealed root.
          • 
      
        • Names, addresses and the operator contact are self-declared; a valid proof shows
        membership in the sealed list, not identity.
