Stay safe

Security notes

  • Fresh address per invoice. Reusing addresses links your customers' payments together. This toolkit derives m/86'/coin'/0'/0/index per invoice — bump the index (or scan) every time.
  • Only confirmed counts. detected means seen on the network (0-conf) — fine for low-value goods, not for anything expensive. Set required confirmations to match the order value.
  • An xpub is public data. Anyone with it can see all your invoice addresses (but can never spend). Keep your seed phrase offline regardless.
  • Verify the domain. Invoice links are only trustworthy from your own site. Bookmark this page; beware lookalike domains.
  • Testnet first. Get free tPRL from the faucet, then run a full payment on testnet before mainnet.
