Pearl Commons

The quadratic funding desk for the Pearl ecosystem. Open a round with a fixed PRL matching pool, register projects, log contributions, and the desk computes each project's match — (Σ√ci)² − Σci, capital-constrained to the pool, sealed in a hash-chained audit log. Keys never touch this page: settlement stays manual, on-chain, in your hands.

Honest limits — always true:
  • The matching pool is a pledged number you enter; the desk does not escrow it and cannot move it. Paying out matches is a manual on-chain transfer by the round operator.
  • Donor labels are self-declared. Repeat donations from one label to one project are aggregated before the square-root step — but the desk cannot prove two labels are two humans. Treat results as best-effort, not sybil-proof.
  • Contributions are logged from what you type or paste; the optional Blockbook txid check reads the chain GET-only and never signs anything.
  • All data lives in this browser's localStorage. Export the round JSON before wiping.
