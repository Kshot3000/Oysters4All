Honest limits

  • Discovery only. This page reads Blockbook and lists what it finds. It never signs, never builds transactions, never broadcasts.
  • Sweep in Pearl Sign. To move found funds, import this same seed into Pearl Sign and spend from there.
  • Your seed stays here. It lives in page memory, is never saved to disk or sent anywhere, and the 🔒 button wipes it instantly.
  • Gap-limit caveat. A chain stops after 20 consecutive unused addresses (configurable). Funds past an unusually long gap won't be seen — raise the gap limit and re-scan if in doubt.
