Honest limits — read before any real PRL moves

  • One transaction, one sender key. Every input must be spendable by the single sender key you sign with. Multi-sender batches are not supported — split them.
  • Dust is refused, never rounded. Any recipient under 546 grains stops the manifest with a loud error. Change under 546 grains is absorbed into the miner fee and disclosed on the review screen — never silently dropped.
  • Memos are off-chain labels. They ride along in the unsigned bundle for your bookkeeping and are verified by the fingerprint — they never appear on-chain. Pearl has no smart contracts and no memo field.
  • The unsigned bundle commits to the final txid. Under segwit rules the txid covers only the non-witness data, so the bundle's fingerprint and txid survive signing unchanged. Import re-verifies everything and refuses loudly on any mismatch — always pair a bundle with its fingerprint.
  • Blockbook is read-only until the single broadcast POST. UTXO scans, fee estimates and tracking are GET-only against the endpoint you configure. If it is unreachable, the page says so — it never presents guesses as data.
  • Secrets stay in this page. Keys you paste live in page memory only, are wiped automatically after signing, and are never written to storage or sent anywhere. Verify every address and amount out of band — start on testnet.
