Honest limits

  • Your key, your coins. This page can only spend what the pasted key controls — and it refuses loudly if the key doesn't derive the analyzed address.
  • Consolidation is final. Once broadcast, the sweep cannot be undone. Check the txid, outputs, and fee on the Sign step before you confirm.
  • Dust is the protocol floor. Outputs below 546 grains are refused; the breakeven guard refuses any plan whose fee eats the recovered value.
  • Thresholds use your fee rate. "Uneconomic" is computed against the fee rate you set — raise the rate and more UTXOs count as uneconomic.
  • Blockbook is a third-party read source. Balances and fee estimates come from the Blockbook you configure; verify anything surprising independently.
  • No funds moved in testing. The builder never executed a live mainnet consolidation; QA runs against stubbed Blockbook responses.
