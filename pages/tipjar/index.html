Honest limits

  • Read-only, always. The widget and this page issue GET-only Blockbook reads. Neither can move funds, sign, or broadcast — tipping happens in the visitor's own wallet via the pearl: URI / QR.
  • Tips count at 1+ confirmations. Unconfirmed mempool transactions are not shown as tips.
  • Dust is not a tip. Outputs below 1,000 grains (0.00001 PRL) are ignored — they're uneconomical to ever spend and would let anyone spam the jar feed for free.
  • Polling cadence. Default every 30 seconds, configurable 15–300s. Be courteous to public Blockbook instances: longer intervals on high-traffic pages.
  • No background notifications. When the tab or page is closed, polling stops — tips that land while you're away appear on the next poll.
  • New-tip detection is per page load. Seen transaction IDs are tracked in page memory. The first successful poll is a silent baseline (existing tips show as history, no confetti); only tips arriving after that get the confetti burst. Reloading the page re-baselines.
  • Descriptor ≠ signature. The tipjar:v1: descriptor proves the config wasn't altered after forging; it doesn't prove who forged it.
