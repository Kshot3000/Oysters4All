Honest limits — payroll edition

  • No auto-pay — ever. The schedule is a planner, not automation. Every payday is a separate manual signed dispatch you approve on the Review step. Pearl has no smart contracts; nothing can move PRL on a timer.
  • One sender key per run. Every input of a pay run must be spendable by the single key you sign with. Multi-key payrolls are not supported — split them.
  • Dust is refused, never rounded. Any payee under 546 grains stops the roster with a loud error. Change under 546 grains is absorbed into the miner fee and disclosed on the review screen — never silently dropped.
  • Labels are off-chain bookkeeping. Payee names ride along in the unsigned bundle and the local history for your records — they never appear on-chain. Pearl transactions carry no memo field.
  • Blockbook is read-only until the single broadcast POST. UTXO scans and fee estimates are GET-only against the endpoint you configure. If it is unreachable, the page says so loudly — it never guesses.
  • Secrets live in page memory only. Keys you paste are wiped automatically after signing, and are never written to storage or sent anywhere. History is this browser's localStorage — exportable to CSV, and yours to clear.
  • Verify addresses out of band. Start on testnet.
