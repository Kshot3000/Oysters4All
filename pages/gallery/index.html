About the wall

Pearlscriptions are Ordinals-style inscriptions on Pearl's Taproot-only chain — witness-envelope artifacts with canonical zero-based numbering. This gallery reads them through the read-only indexer API contract (GET-only): /inscriptions, /inscriptions/:id, /inscriptions/:id/content, /inscriptions/:id/location, /addresses/:address/inscriptions.

Safety first: only raster images, audio and video are rendered inline. HTML and SVG are never executed — they're shown as text or hex. Oversize content (>8 MB) is never previewed. Previews load lazily as frames scroll into view.
