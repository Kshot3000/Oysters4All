Convene the council

Name the vault, seat 1–5 cosigners by their public keys (x-only 64-hex, or compressed 66-hex — normalized in-browser), and set the threshold m. The desk forges one CHECKSIGADD tapscript leaf (0 <K1> CHECKSIGADD … <Kn> CHECKSIGADD <m> EQUAL) sealed under a NUMS internal key — nobody knows its discrete log, so there is no keypath backdoor: coins move only through the quorum script. No secrets are collected here; keys stay with their owners.

Draft the motion

Choose the vault's UTXOs to spend, name the recipients, and set a fee rate. The desk builds an unsigned bundle (pearl-quorum-unsigned:v1:) carrying a 64-bit fingerprint and the per-input BIP-341 script-path sighash (SIGHASH_DEFAULT). Change returns to the vault; dust change is absorbed into the fee and disclosed. Nothing is signed here.

    Honest limits

    • Script-path multisig, not MuSig. Every signer produces their own Schnorr signature over the same sighash, and the witness carries all m signatures plus the full script. A quorum spend is therefore larger and costlier than a MuSig keypath spend of the same threshold. What you gain: no interactive key-setup ceremony, and every signature is independently verifiable against its cosigner's key.
    • Cosigners must be real, distinct parties holding their own keys. The desk refuses duplicate keys, but it cannot tell whether two "cosigners" are the same person with two keypairs. A 2-of-3 where one person holds two keys is a 1-of-1 wearing a costume.
    • Verify the vault address independently. Before funding, recompute the address from the descriptor on a second machine (or with the recompute button) and confirm every cosigner sees the same address. A wrong address means the money is gone.
    • Designated-cosigner internal keys are a backdoor. NUMS is the default and has no keypath spend. If you choose a cosigner's key as the internal key, that cosigner can spend unilaterally — the desk stamps the warning on the descriptor and every screen.
    • Keys. Pasted private keys live in memory only and are wiped after signing. Prefer signing on an offline machine and importing the signature bundle.
    • Fees move. The fee rate is an estimate at planning time; a broadcast tx can linger if the mempool is hot. No real broadcast is performed by any test.
