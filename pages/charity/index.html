Pearl Charity

Launch a transparent PRL donation campaign. Donations go straight to the charity's own address — this page never holds, moves, or broadcasts funds. Every campaign carries a tamper-evident descriptor and a 64-bit fingerprint; every donor receipt is hash-bound and printable. Verify everything standalone.

Honest limits — always true:
  • This page never holds or moves funds. Donations travel from the donor's own wallet to the charity's address. There is no escrow, no vault, no smart contract.
  • A campaign is organizer-trusted: the descriptor proves the campaign text was not altered, but trust in the recipient address is social — verify the organizer independently before donating.
  • Receipt donor names are self-declared — a receipt proves a transaction happened, not who sent it.
  • Deadlines are absolute block heights (exact). Day-estimates use ≈194 s per block and are labeled approximate — the chain decides, not the clock.
  • Funding checks read Blockbook, a third-party indexer — it can lag or be wrong. Receipts stay valid offline; only their live cross-check needs Blockbook.
  • The page never broadcasts. No real donation was made in testing.

Launch a campaign

Forge a tamper-evident campaign. The recipient address is validated in-browser and committed into the descriptor hash — anyone who alters it later fails verification loudly. Optionally sign the campaign with an organizer key (held in memory only, wiped right after forging).

    Verify

    Two standalone verifiers. They recompute everything from the descriptor/spec and refuse loudly on any mismatch — never trust, always re-derive.

    Campaign check

    Paste a campaign descriptor and its spec. The verifier recomputes the hash, fingerprint, and recipient address — and refuses if the claimed address does not match. If the campaign was signed, the organizer's Schnorr signature is checked too.

    Receipt check

    Paste a receipt JSON. The verifier recomputes its hash — then, optionally, cross-checks the donation against Blockbook (tx exists, pays the campaign address the recorded amount, ≥ 1 confirmation).
