Sign Pearl transactions without trusting a server.

A five-step forge for Pearl (PRL) Taproot transactions: derive keys offline, assemble coins, build the exact wire bytes, sign every input with BIP-341 keypath Schnorr, re-verify each signature locally — then broadcast only when you're satisfied. Everything runs in this page; the vendored crypto has no network calls.

Air-gap friendly. Once this page is loaded, it works with no internet: save it to a USB stick, open it on an offline machine, and carry unsigned/signed hex back and forth on paper or QR. The signing key never has to touch a networked device.

Honest limits

    
    
      
  • Keypath spends only — P2TR addresses (prl1p…). Script-path and multisig are not supported in this version.
    • 
      
  • Fee estimates come from the blockbook you configure; if it's unreachable, enter a manual rate.
    • 
      
  • A "verified" signature proves the math checks out against the prevouts you supplied — it can't prove those prevouts are real or unspent. Confirm UTXOs against your own node before moving serious money.
    • 
      
  • Pasting a mnemonic or WIF into any browser makes it a hot key. The air-gapped flow (watch-only build here → sign offline) exists for a reason.
