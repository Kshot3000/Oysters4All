Pearl · Fees Desk

Pearl Fees

The fee-rate desk for PRL. Point it at your pearld node's RPC and it reads the live mempool, ranks every transaction by grains per vbyte, and tells you what to pay for next-block, ~10-minute, ~1-hour, or economy confirmation. Plan P2TR transaction sizes, and build an honest fee-bump plan for a stuck transaction. Read-only — keys never touch this page.

Honest limits — always true:
  • Pearl has no smart contracts and no fee market oracle: rates come from your own node's mempool via getrawmempool(verbose=true), or from clearly-labeled sample data when no node is connected.
  • Confirmation estimates are a rough model (fee-rate-priority packing into ~1 MB blocks, ~3m14s block target). Miners may order transactions however they like — treat every ETA as a guess, not a promise.
  • The bump planner plans numbers only — it never signs, never broadcasts, and cannot see your wallet. RBF only works if the stuck tx signaled it and your node's policy accepts replacements.
  • RPC credentials stay in this browser's localStorage. Nothing is sent anywhere except to the endpoint you configure.

Stuck-transaction bump planner

Paste a stuck transaction's txid. The desk fetches it from your connected node, measures its exact vsize from the raw bytes, and tells you what a replacement (RBF) or CPFP child must pay to reach your target rate — plus whether the original even signaled RBF.

Node connection

Pearl's RPC is disabled by default. Enable it in pearld.conf with rpcuser / rpcpass (RPC listens on port 44107 mainnet / 44109 testnet, TLS on by default). Everything stays between this page and your node.

Leave the endpoint empty and the desk runs on clearly-labeled sample data — the estimator and math all still work.
